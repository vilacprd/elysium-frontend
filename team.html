Get to know us

The Chef

A passionate advocate for authenticity and quality products, he conveys his roots through honest and constantly evolving cuisine. After training at the Culinary Institute of America in New York, he opened his flagship restaurant, Pujol, at the young age of 24. In 2014, he made a significant impact in New York with Cosme, whose success led him to open Atla three years later. Recently, he has opened the doors to his third venture in the United States, Damian in Los Angeles, California. Olvera is the author of several internationally published gastronomic books. Additionally, his passion for preserving Mexico's natural heritage, as well as his awareness and respect for its biodiversity, is evident in both his work as a chef and his writings on sustainability, food, and social responsibility.

Enrique Olvera, a native of Mexico City, is a culinary virtuoso whose journey began in the bustling kitchens of his grandmother's home. Inspired by the rich tapestry of flavors and traditions passed down through generations, Enrique embarked on a lifelong quest for culinary excellence. After honing his skills in some of the most prestigious kitchens across Europe, Enrique's talent and dedication earned him the opportunity to lead his own restaurant. In 2010, he opened the doors to his eponymous establishment, "Elysium," in the heart of Granada. With an unwavering commitment to quality and innovation, Enrique quickly garnered attention from critics and diners alike. Within a year of its opening, "Elysium" was awarded its first Michelin star, a testament to Enrique's mastery of the culinary arts.

Beyond the accolades and recognition, Enrique remains grounded in his passion for creating unforgettable dining experiences that celebrate the vibrant flavors of Mexican cuisine. His commitment to excellence, coupled with his humility and generosity, has earned him the admiration and respect of colleagues and patrons alike. Today, Elysium's culinary empire continues to thrive, with "Elysium" serving as the flagship of his gastronomic legacy in Granada. As he looks to the future, Enrique remains committed to pushing the boundaries of culinary innovation while staying true to the timeless traditions that have shaped his remarkable journey.
