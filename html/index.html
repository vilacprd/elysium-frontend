We live between tradition and gastronomic modernity, reinventing those deeply Granadian dishes that my family prepared in the old inn, to adapt them to the flavors and style that we are so passionate today. Enrique Olvera

Our Story

The kitchen? No way! Surprised? It was madness! My parents lived and breathed for Elypsium. And I, every day, when I got back from school as a kid, would walk through the tavern full of customers, pass by the bar, and sit quietly in the kitchen to do my homework. That constant racket, that smell... All I wanted was to become the next Mick Jagger or master the ball like Pirri! But, neither happened because I couldn't kick the ball with my legs and God didn't give me a voice. Additionally, being a chef wasn't fashionable back then. It didn't have the glamour it has today. But life takes many turns, and here I am, the owner of Elypsium.

We celebrate quality and innovation

Awarded with two Michelin Stars, Elysium celebrates the rich gastronomic tradition of southern Spain by reimagining it with creativity and a unique touch. Our culinary approach is deeply rooted in the use of high-quality, locally sourced, and seasonal ingredients, ensuring each dish is a testament to freshness and flavor.
Our chefs combine traditional techniques with innovative methods, crafting dishes that not only honor the heritage of Andalusian cuisine but also push the boundaries of contemporary gastronomy. At Elysium, every meal is a journey through the vibrant tastes and textures of southern Spain, presented with elegance and an artistic flair that reflects our commitment to excellence and our passion for culinary artistry.

Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication, and our restaurant embodies this philosophy in every dish. Our dishes are a work of art, combining minimalist design with exquisite flavors that captivate the palate. Each plate is carefully crafted to highlight the essence of its ingredients, showcasing their natural beauty and taste.

Our commitment to using the finest, locally sourced ingredients ensures that every meal is not only a feast for the senses but also a celebration of the season's bounty. Join us for an unforgettable dining experience that transcends the ordinary and redefines sophisticated simplicity.

Elysium Sustainable

The Elysium restaurant was conceived and built with sustainability as a priority, a fundamental pillar of Eneko Atxa's philosophy. The design of the building creates a space where boundaries are broken and modern architecture blends with nature, extending inside and across the external roof where it houses the sustainability center

Three Michelin Stars

In the 2012/2013 Michelin Guide, Elysium is awarded three stars. It leaves behind its 2012 season "The Taste of the Mediterranean" and begins 2013 with "Made in the Moon." It continues to be listed among the 50 best restaurants in the world by the prestigious magazine "The Restaurant." This recognition solidifies its reputation for innovative and exceptional cuisine. Guests can look forward to a dining experience that seamlessly blends creativity with the finest ingredients.
