Wagyu Sirloin with Truffled Mashed Potatoes and Grilled Asparagus

Indulge in an exceptional culinary experience with our Wagyu sirloin,a delight
of the most tender and flavorful meat, sourced from the finest, carefully
selected cattle. This exquisite cut is served perfectly cooked and accompanied
by luxurious truffled mashed potatoes, crafted with the most aromatic black
truffles, adding a touch of indulgence to every bite.

Wild Alaskan Salmon with Lobster Risotto and White Asparagus

Let yourself be seduced by the freshness and refinement of wild Alaskan salmon
in this dish that combines the nobility of fish with the indulgence of lobster.
The salmon, carefully selected and prepared by our expert chefs,
is delicately cooked and complemented by a luxurious lobster risotto,
where each grain of rice is infused with the rich and seductive flavor of the sea.

Cod Fillet with Lemon Risotto and Crispy Leeks

Savor the delicate flavors of our cod fillet, expertly prepared and served atop
a bed of creamy lemon risotto. The cod, known for its tender texture and mild taste,
is gently poached to perfection, allowing its natural sweetness to shine through.
The risotto, infused with bright citrus notes, adds a refreshing contrast
to the richness of the fish, creating a harmonious balance of flavors.
